Selected Projects

ULTRA-2.5D

An analytical placement framework for ultra-large-scale, multi-reticle 2.5D chiplet systems. The framework combines partitioning, differentiable global placement, and constraint-aware legalization to jointly optimize chiplet positions, rotations, and flips.

  • Partitioning: MIQP-based assignment considering connectivity, critical nets, and area utilization.
  • Global placement: Gumbel-softmax relaxation of discrete orientations, with GPU acceleration.
  • Legalization: Constraint graphs and linear programming to remove overlaps and satisfy boundary constraints.

Analog Circuit Parameter Optimization with Python and LTSpice

Undergraduate graduation project

A simulation-based tool for optimizing analog circuit parameters. It integrates simulated annealing (SA), differential evolution (DE), genetic algorithms (GA), particle swarm optimization (PSO), Bayesian optimization (BO), and two improved algorithms.

The graphical interface provides controls for selecting the optimization method, opening a simulation file, choosing a process library, and starting or stopping an optimization run.
