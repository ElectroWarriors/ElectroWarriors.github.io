Jinhong Huang

I am a Ph.D. student at Fudan University, in the College of Integrated Circuits & Micro-Nano Electronics and the State Key Laboratory of Integrated Chips and Systems. I am a member of CODA lab, which I joined in Fall 2024, working with Prof. Keren Zhu and Prof. Xuan Zeng. I received my B.S. degree from the College of Physics and Information Engineering (College of Microelectronics) at Fuzhou University.

My research focuses on electronic design automation for 2.5D chiplet systems, particularly analytical placement for large-scale, multi-reticle designs.
